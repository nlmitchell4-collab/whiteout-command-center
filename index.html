Foundry Overview

Foundry planning is built around objective control, not scattered fighting. The assignment engine prioritizes high-value buildings, keeps secondary targets close to each combatant's primary objective and limits overcrowding by capping assignments per building based on priority.

Phase 1 emphasizes Boiler, Transit and both Prototypes. Phase 2 shifts attention toward Mercenary Camp, Munitions, Boiler, Imperial Foundry and Prototypes. Phase 3 keeps Imperial Foundry and Boiler at the top while maintaining Munitions, Mercenary Camp, Prototypes and nearby support buildings.

Rules & Best Practices

  • Capture buildings before chasing fights; points and buffs compound faster than random trades.
  • Do not overload one building. Critical objectives can carry more combatants, but every available objective should have coverage first.
  • Keep each combatant's secondary and tertiary objectives near their primary objective so rotations are realistic.
  • Lower-power combatants should lean toward east-side safe-zone objectives when possible.
  • In Phase 1, secure Boiler and Transit quickly while contesting both Prototypes.
  • In Phase 2, prioritize Mercenary Camp, Munitions and Boiler while keeping pressure on Imperial Foundry and both Prototypes.
  • In Phase 3, Imperial Foundry and Boiler are the primary anchors; Munitions, Mercenary Camp, Prototypes, Workshops and Repairs support the endgame.

Canyon Overview

Canyon Clash planning uses the shared roster and the Canyon Assignment field. Each roster legion is assigned to one Canyon map from the Import page, then divided into Blue, Green, Red and Yellow route teams that move together.

The battlefield map now highlights phase-specific routes instead of individual capture labels. Route directions are relative to the assigned Canyon map, so the same path may be northern from Stormrage, western from Iceguard, or flipped from Flameguard.

Blue and Red push into enemy territory on opposite outside paths, while Yellow and Green control the center routes and prepare the Citadel collapse.

Admins can set the Canyon map and optional team leaders on the Import page. If leaders are not selected, the highest troop-power combatants become leaders automatically.

Canyon Battlefield

Canyon Rules & Best Practices

  • Mark Canyon participants in the roster editor before the match; No engagement players are excluded from team balancing.
  • The highest eligible troop-power players become Blue, Green, Red and Yellow leaders unless Import page leader overrides are set.
  • Remaining players are assigned to the smallest and lowest-power team first so teams stay even.
  • Read route directions from the assigned territory. For example, Stormrage's northern route is Iceguard's western route.
  • Blue and Red follow opposite outside enemy-push routes based on the assigned territory.
  • Yellow and Green control the two center routes based on the assigned territory.
  • Teams should move together along the highlighted phase route instead of splitting across unrelated buildings.
  • Use the Import page Canyon settings to assign Legion 1 and Legion 2 to their WOS Canyon maps.
  • During Fortress Occupation, outside teams continue enemy-side pressure while center teams secure home-side access lanes.
  • During Citadel Onslaught, teams collapse from their route into Frozen Citadel with their leader instead of arriving one by one.
